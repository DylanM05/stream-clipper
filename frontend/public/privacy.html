Privacy policy

Stream Clipper · last updated Oct 24, 2026

Stream Clipper is a private tool that its operator runs on their own server. It turns stream recordings into clips and uploads them to YouTube channels the operator manages, with the permission of the channels' owners. It isn't offered to the public.

YouTube API Services

Stream Clipper uses YouTube API Services to upload videos and add them to playlists. By connecting a YouTube channel to it, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

What it accesses and why

When a channel is connected through Google's sign-in, Stream Clipper gets permission to:

  • Upload videos to that channel, with the title, description and visibility set in the app.
  • See and create the channel's playlists, and add videos to them, so uploads can go into the playlist picked in the app. Google grants this as permission to manage the YouTube account; Stream Clipper uses it for nothing else.
  • See the channel's ID and name, to check that the right channel was connected.

It doesn't read anything else from your Google or YouTube account, and never changes or deletes existing videos.

What it stores

  • A Google sign-in token for each connected channel, plus the channel's ID and name, kept in a database on the operator's server. The token is used only to upload videos the operator starts and manage their playlists.
  • The videos it uploads, with their titles and descriptions. Video files are deleted from the server soon after they're uploaded; titles, descriptions and links to the uploaded videos are kept as a record.
  • One cookie that keeps the operator signed in to the app.

Nothing is sold, shared with anyone else, or used for advertising.

Removing access

Disconnecting a channel in the app deletes its sign-in token and revokes it with Google. You can also remove Stream Clipper's access at any time from your Google Account's third-party connections page.

Contact

Questions go to the operator, at the support email shown on Google's sign-in screen for this app.
